Create a complete, polished, highly immersive VR game for Meta Quest 3 called **GREEN LANTERN: WILLPOWER VR**.

This must be designed as a **full Green Lantern power-fantasy VR experience** where the player truly feels like a Green Lantern and can **create, summon, control, and fight with a wide variety of Green Lantern constructs**. The game should feel like the player has been recruited into the Green Lantern Corps and is now using a power ring to protect Oa, train in the use of willpower-based constructs, travel through space, and fight enemies using creative hard-light manifestations.

Do not treat this as a vague superhero concept. Treat it as a full production-ready VR game concept and produce a strong build spec. The output should define the game clearly enough that the downstream pipeline can generate strong assets, environments, UI elements, interactions, and gameplay systems.

---

# 1. CORE GAME CONCEPT

## Game Title
**GREEN LANTERN: WILLPOWER VR**

## Core Fantasy
The player becomes a Green Lantern and uses a power ring in first-person VR to:
- Explore Oa and nearby space sectors
- Train as a Lantern recruit
- Create and wield hard-light constructs
- Fly, hover, and navigate in zero-gravity and planetary environments
- Defend civilians and allies
- Battle robotic, alien, and fear-based enemies
- Recharge at Lantern power stations
- Progress from a new recruit to a fully capable space guardian

## Player Role
The player is an **original Green Lantern recruit**, newly chosen by a ring and brought to **Oa**, headquarters of the Green Lantern Corps. This keeps the player as the main hero while still allowing the world to include the broader Green Lantern mythology and atmosphere.

The player is not a passive observer. The player is the active Green Lantern. The player’s body, ring hand, constructs, movement, and combat should all reinforce this fantasy.

## Tone
- Heroic
- Empowering
- Cosmic
- High-tech
- Mythic sci-fi
- Vibrant emerald energy aesthetic
- Cinematic comic-book energy, but immersive and physically interactive in VR

## Visual Style
Use a **stylized cinematic comic-book sci-fi look** with:
- Strong silhouettes
- Clean readable forms
- Bright emerald hard-light energy
- Smooth dark suit materials with luminous green accents
- Monumental alien architecture on Oa
- Starfields, nebulae, planetary horizons, and energy structures
- Readable, image-to-3D-friendly asset designs
- Avoid overcomplicated texture noise on key assets
- Prioritize bold shapes and distinct material separation so generated images convert well into 3D models

# 2. HIGH-LEVEL GAME STRUCTURE

## Game Structure Overview
The game should be structured around:
1. **Arrival on Oa**
2. **Training and construct mastery**
3. **Patrol missions and sector defense**
4. **Advanced combat and mobility challenges**
5. **Major Oa defense scenario / final mission**

## Primary Gameplay Pillars
1. **Construct Creation**
2. **VR Combat**
3. **Flight and Hovering**
4. **Exploration**
5. **Mission-Based Progression**
6. **Power Management / Recharge**
7. **Willpower Mastery**

## Core Gameplay Loop
The main loop should be:
- Spawn in Oa hub
- Receive mission briefing from ring interface or Corps command console
- Travel to training space or active mission zone
- Use movement, flying, and constructs to complete objectives
- Defeat enemies or solve mission scenarios
- Earn willpower experience, unlock new constructs, and upgrade mastery
- Return to Oa or proceed to next mission
- Recharge and prepare for the next deployment

- Like they can intelligently choose different constructs for different situations
- Like flight and combat are empowering, not passive

- Like the power ring is both a weapon and a tool

- Form giant fists and smash enemies

- Create platforms in midair
- Form a cage around enemies

# 5. PLAYER MOVEMENT, VR BODY LOGIC, AND COMFORT

## Core Movement Modes
The player must have:
1. **Ground locomotion**
2. **Hover mode**
3. **Full flight mode**
4. **Contextual traversal using constructs**

## Ground Movement
- First-person locomotion using left thumbstick
- Comfortable default walk speed
- Optional sprint burst
- Snap turn by default with optional smooth turn
- Optional vignette comfort setting
- Smooth locomotion must feel stable and readable

## Hover Mode
- The player can enter a controlled hovering state
- Hover mode is slower and more precise than full flight
- Used for combat positioning, fine navigation, and construct placement
- Should give subtle floating body feedback and gentle movement drift correction

## Flight Mode
Flight is a core fantasy and must feel powerful but controllable.
- Flight should be available in large open areas and mission spaces
- The player can transition from standing or hovering into flight
- Flight should feel like controlled propulsion from ring power
- Allow a comfortable cruising speed and a boost mode
- Include inertia, but keep it manageable for comfort
- Support quick stopping into hover mode
- Flight should work well both in atmosphere and in open-space combat areas

### Recommended Flight Feel
- Empowering but not nausea-inducing
- Smooth acceleration
- Strong visual feedback from green energy trails
- Gentle camera stabilization
- Optional comfort assistance for tight turns

## Physical Presence
The player should see:
- Their ring hand
- Their gloved arms
- A subtle Lantern suit body presence when looking down if needed
- Construct energy emerging from the ring hand

# 6. QUEST 3 VR CONTROLS AND INTERACTION MODEL

Design controls around Quest 3 Touch Plus-style behavior. The goal is clarity and responsiveness.

## Left Controller
- **Left Thumbstick**
  - Move on ground
  - Strafe while hovering
  - In flight: move forward/back/strafe relative to chosen locomotion setting
- **Left Thumbstick Click**
  - Sprint on ground
  - Boost modifier in flight
- **X Button**
  - Open wrist menu / mission log / settings panel
  - Tap to cycle HUD modes if menu is closed
- **Y Button**
  - Hold to open the construct category wheel
  - Release on a category to select active construct family
- **Left Trigger**
  - Secondary ability / target assist / contextual support action
  - Can be used to lock or refine selected construct behavior
- **Left Grip**
  - Grab physical objects
  - Stabilize two-handed constructs
  - Pull, hold, or manipulate world elements when allowed

## Right Controller
- **Right Thumbstick**
  - Snap or smooth turn left/right
  - In flight, up/down adjusts altitude or pitch assist depending on mode
- **A Button**
  - Interact / confirm / context action
  - Double-tap to toggle hover or flight where allowed
- **B Button**
  - Cancel / dismiss active preview / quick evade burst
  - Hold to dismiss current sustained construct
- **Right Trigger**
  - Primary construct activation
  - Fire energy blast
  - Confirm spawn of selected construct
  - Hold to sustain beam or charge a heavy attack
- **Right Grip**
  - Ring shaping mode
  - Grab with dominant hand
  - Hold for freeform construct shaping or orientation control

## Two-Handed Behavior
When both hands are used together:
- Larger constructs can be scaled or expanded
- Heavier objects can be grabbed or moved
- Some advanced constructs require two-hand stability
- Holding both grips while manipulating a construct should allow the player to size, rotate, or anchor it

## Haptics
- Light pulse when hovering over interactables
- Sharp pulse when firing energy blasts
- Heavy burst when construct fully forms
- Rhythmic resonance during charging
- Strong impact feedback when blocking or colliding
- Recharge station should produce steady escalating haptics during ring restoration

# 7. CONSTRUCT SYSTEM – THE MAIN GAME MECHANIC

This is the most important system in the game.

The player must be able to create multiple types of Green Lantern constructs in VR. The system should feel flexible, readable, and empowering, while still being mechanically usable.

## Construct Design Goals
- Easy to understand in VR
- Fast enough for combat
- Flexible enough to feel creative
- Visually iconic
- Stable enough for game progression
- Designed so downstream image generation can create clear model references

## Construct Categories
Create at least the following core construct categories:

1. **Basic Attack Constructs**
   - Energy bolt
   - Giant fist
   - Hammer
   - Sword
   - Spear
   - Blaster / cannon

2. **Defense Constructs**
   - Round shield
   - Wide barrier wall
   - Dome shield
   - Armor overlay / reinforced guard mode

3. **Control Constructs**
   - Energy cage
   - Chain / tether
   - Restraining bands
   - Grapple anchor

4. **Traversal Constructs**
   - Platform
   - Bridge segment
   - Ladder / stepping projection
   - Ram / battering surf construct
   - Temporary floating path pieces

5. **Utility Constructs**
   - Scanner drone
   - Grabber hand
   - Repair tool arm
   - Searchlight / projection beacon
   - Shield bubble for civilians

6. **Heavy / Signature Constructs**
   - Giant gauntlet
   - Turret
   - Mech-like oversized arm
   - Multi-part battering ram
   - Energy jet or drill
   - Construct swarm / multiple fists

## Construct Usage Modes
There should be three layers of construct usage:

### A. Instant Constructs
Quick-use constructs for combat or defense.
- Selected from construct wheel
- Spawn rapidly
- Examples: fist, shield, sword, barrier

### B. Sustained Constructs
Remain active while the player channels willpower.
- Drain ring energy over time
- Examples: shield dome, cage, beam, bridge, tether

### C. Freeform / Guided Custom Constructs
A simplified “creative” layer where the player can form broader shapes without needing artistic perfection.
- Player holds shaping mode
- A wireframe preview appears from the ring
- Hand movement defines general size/orientation
- System interprets intent into clean usable shapes
- Best for walls, platforms, blunt weapons, simple tools
- The system should prioritize playability over raw freehand complexity

## Construct Selection Flow
- Hold **Y** to open radial construct wheel
- Categories appear as holographic icons around the player’s ring hand
- Point at a category with the dominant hand or gaze
- Release **Y** to equip
- Use **Right Trigger** to summon the selected construct
- Use **Grip** to modify scale/orientation while previewing
- Release to finalize

## Construct Preview Behavior
Before finalizing a construct:
- Show translucent green hologram preview
- Show valid placement guides if construct is placeable
- Show size indicator if scalable
- If an enemy can be targeted, show targeting highlight
- Invalid placements should turn amber or flicker

## Construct Resource Rules
Constructs should use:
- **Ring Charge** as primary energy resource
- **Willpower Meter** or mastery rating for advanced construct stability

Rules:
- Basic constructs cost little
- Heavy constructs cost more
- Sustained constructs drain energy over time
- Larger constructs cost more than smaller ones
- Overuse can temporarily reduce construct strength or cause cooldown delays

## Construct Feel
Constructs should:
- Appear as luminous hard-light objects
- Have slightly translucent emerald energy with brighter edges
- Form from swirling ring energy and geometric light lines
- Emit glow, particle sparks, and energy traces during creation
- Look strong and clean, not messy or smoky

# 8. COMBAT SYSTEM

Combat should feel dynamic, expressive, and physically involved.

## Core Combat Actions
- Fire energy shots
- Summon melee constructs
- Block with shields
- Trap enemies with cages or tethers
- Launch heavy power attacks
- Attack while hovering or flying
- Use environmental constructs for tactical advantage

## Damage Model
Player has:
- **Health**
- **Ring Charge**
- Optional **Willpower Focus** meter for advanced skills

Health is reduced by enemy attacks.
Ring Charge is reduced by construct use and some enemy interference.
If Ring Charge is too low, only weak emergency constructs are available until recharge.

## Combat Flow
Moment-to-moment combat should involve:
- Reading enemy attacks
- Choosing correct construct type
- Aiming or placing the construct
- Timing shields or evasions
- Closing distance or controlling space
- Managing ring energy
- Switching from ranged to melee or utility constructs fluidly

## Melee Combat
Melee constructs should feel physical and satisfying.
Examples:
- Giant fist punch
- Sword slash
- Hammer smash
- Spear thrust

The player should swing or thrust naturally in VR, but the game should provide aim assistance and readable hit detection so it feels strong rather than awkward.

## Ranged Combat
- Energy bolts and beams fired from ring hand
- Charged shot for stronger blast
- Turret and cannon constructs as advanced options

## Defense
- Shield raised by summon action
- Barrier wall placed in world
- Dome shield for protection zones
- Timed defense can produce a stronger block effect or reflect projectiles

## Crowd Control
- Tether enemies
- Cage single targets
- Sweep multiple foes with ram or wide constructs
- Knockback with giant gauntlet or hammer

## Aerial Combat
Enemies can also fly or hover.
The player should be able to:
- Track aerial enemies
- Fire while hovering
- Boost to reposition
- Use constructs in 360-degree space
- Defend from ranged attacks while in the air

# 9. ENEMIES AND NPCS

## Enemy Factions
Use a mix of enemy types that suit Green Lantern gameplay.

### 1. Manhunter-Type Combat Drones
- Robotic enemies
- Angular, metallic, intimidating
- Red sensor lights or hostile glows
- Use blasters, shields, or hover movement
- Great early and mid-game enemies

### 2. Fear-Energy Raiders
- Yellow-energy themed enemies
- Hostile soldiers, drones, or creatures using fear-based tech or energy
- Designed as direct contrast to green constructs
- Use ranged attacks, suppression beams, and disruptive projectiles

### 3. Alien Pirates / Sector Criminals
- Organic or armored humanoid enemies
- Use tech weapons
- Good for patrol missions and rescue scenarios

### 4. Heavy Brutes
- Large armored enemies
- Require strong constructs, flanking, or restraint
- Great targets for giant fist, hammer, or cage mechanics

### 5. Boss Enemies
At least 2–3 major boss archetypes:
- Massive drone commander
- Fear-energy construct beast
- Elite rival construct user

## Enemy Behavior
Enemies should:
- Telegraph attacks clearly
- Take cover or reposition
- Fly or hover where appropriate
- Pressure the player from multiple ranges
- Force construct choice diversity
- React visibly when trapped, staggered, or blocked

## NPC Roles
Include:
- Oa trainers
- Corps command officers
- Civilian rescue targets
- Technicians / lore NPCs
- Holographic tutors explaining constructs

NPCs should reinforce the world and provide mission context, but the player remains the hero.

# 12. POWER, RECHARGE, FAIL STATES, AND SAVE SYSTEMS

## Ring Charge
Central mechanical resource.
- Used by nearly all constructs
- Refills partially over time at low level
- Fully refilled at recharge stations
- Special stations on Oa and in some missions

## Recharge Interaction
Recharge should feel ceremonial and satisfying.
- Player places ring hand into or near recharge node
- Green energy surges through ring
- Voice input can optionally be used for a Lantern-style recharge declaration or generic pledge phrase
- Do not require exact copyrighted oath wording
- Strong visual beam effect and haptics

## Fail States
Player can fail if:
- Health reaches zero
- Critical mission target is destroyed
- Too many civilians are lost
- Timed objective expires
- Ring charge fully depletes in certain high-risk mission contexts

## Checkpoints
- Mid-mission checkpoints
- Mission restart option
- Oa hub saves progression persistently

## Rewards
- Construct unlocks
- Cosmetic suit variations
- Ring efficiency upgrades
- Mastery ranks
- Score medals for training or mission performance

# 15. NON-NEGOTIABLE EXPERIENCE REQUIREMENTS

The final game concept must preserve these key experience requirements:
1. The player must truly feel like a Green Lantern in VR.
2. Construct creation must be the centerpiece of the gameplay.
3. Flight, hovering, combat, and exploration must all be part of the experience.
4. Oa must exist as a major explorable and memorable location.
5. The game must support both combat constructs and utility constructs.
6. Quest 3 controls must be mapped clearly and used intentionally.
7. The build spec must be detailed enough to drive downstream asset generation.
8. Major assets must be visually distinct and easy for image generation and image-to-3D workflows to interpret.
9. The game should feel complete, not like a tech demo.
10. The tone should be heroic, cosmic, and empowering.

# 16. FINAL CREATIVE DIRECTION SUMMARY

This game should deliver the fantasy of being a Green Lantern in VR:
- flying through cosmic environments,
- defending Oa,
- manifesting willpower into hard-light tools and weapons,
- solving problems with creativity,
- battling enemies across the ground and in the air,
- and growing into a true member of the Green Lantern Corps.

The build spec should prioritize:
- strong player fantasy,
- rich VR interaction,
- readable asset generation,
- construct variety,
- satisfying combat,
- and memorable science-fantasy worldbuilding.
